How it works

Four steps, and no surprises at the end.

  1. 01

    Send the picture

    A screenshot, a magazine page or a photo of an old piece all work. Tell us roughly what weight or budget you have in mind.

  2. 02

    We quote it

    Three numbers come back, written down before anything starts: gold weight, making charge, wastage. If the design needs changing to work in gold, we will show you what changes.

  3. 03

    Advance and making

    Work starts on an advance. Most pieces take one to three weeks, and bridal sets in wedding season need longer. Come early rather than late.

  4. 04

    Weighed and handed over

    The finished piece goes on the scale in front of you, carrying its purity stamp. Metal, making and wastage appear as separate lines on the bill.

What helps us

Send these and the quote comes back faster.

  • A clear photograph of the design, front on if you can
  • Anything for scale, such as a coin or a finger in the shot
  • The occasion and the date you need it by
  • A weight or a budget, even a rough one
  • Whether you want 22 carat or 18 carat
  • Old gold you want to put towards it
