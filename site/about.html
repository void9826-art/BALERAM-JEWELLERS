About the shop

A jeweller in the
Aeroplane Building.

We sell gold and silver in Nepalgunj, to families who mostly come back. Weddings, births, festivals, and the pieces people buy slowly over years. The counters are open and the scale sits where you can read it.

Why people come back

Serving customers with a peaceful environment.

That line is the shop’s own. A gold purchase is rarely a small decision, and it should happen sitting down, with as many trays as it takes.

  • Bridal sittings with the full set tried on together
  • Old gold tested and exchanged while you watch
  • Repairs, polishing, re-sizing and re-stringing
  • Pieces made to a design you bring in
  • Silver for the house, for gifting and for puja
