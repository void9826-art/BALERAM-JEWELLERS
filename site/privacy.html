Privacy

What this website
knows about you.

Almost nothing. This page says exactly what happens, in plain words, so you do not have to take it on faith.

In detail

No accounts, no tracking, no cookies from us.

Does this site set cookies?

No. This website sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. There is no account to create, no login, and no basket. That is why you have not been shown a cookie banner: there is nothing to ask you to agree to.

Do you collect my details?

There is no contact form on this website, so there is nothing here that can collect your name, your email address or your phone number. Every button either opens WhatsApp or dials the shop.

What happens when I press WhatsApp?

Your own WhatsApp app opens, with a message already typed for you. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself. From that point the conversation is between you and the shop, and WhatsApp handles it under their own privacy terms, not ours. We keep those chats to answer you and to remember an order you placed.

What about the map on the About page?

The map is loaded from Google, and Google does set cookies. That is why the map does not load on its own. You see a still placeholder with a button, and nothing is requested from Google until you press it. If you never press it, Google is never contacted.

Are there any other outside companies involved?

The typefaces used on this site are served from this website itself rather than from Google, so no font request leaves for another company. The only outside links are the ones you choose to press: WhatsApp, the phone dialler, and Google Maps for directions.

Is there analytics?

Not at the moment. If visitor counting is ever added, this page will be updated first and anything that sets a cookie will ask you before it runs.

How do I ask about my information?

Message the shop on WhatsApp or call 982 4540992 and ask. If you want a WhatsApp conversation deleted from our side, say so and it will be.
